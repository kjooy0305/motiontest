① 명사 Noun

📌
사람·사물·장소·개념의 이름. 문장에서 주어·목적어·보어 역할을 합니다.
종류설명예
가산명사 (Countable)셀 수 있음 · a/an 붙일 수 있음a book, two cats, an apple
불가산명사 (Uncountable)셀 수 없음 · a/an 불가 · 단수 동사water, music, advice, information

집합명사 (Collective)구성원의 집단team, family, class, staff
추상명사 (Abstract)눈에 보이지 않는 개념love, happiness, freedom, hope

현재 시제 Present Tenses

🔄현재 단순 Simple Present
용법: 습관·반복 행동, 불변의 사실·진리, 감정·상태
주어 + 동사원형 (3인칭 단수: +s/es)
She drinks coffee every morning. (습관)
Water boils at 100°C. (진리)
빈도부사와 자주 함께 씀: always, usually, often, sometimes, never
▶️현재 진행 Present Continuous
용법: 지금 진행 중, 가까운 미래 계획
am/is/are + V-ing
I am studying right now. (진행)
We are meeting tomorrow. (예정된 미래)
상태동사는 진행형 불가: know, like, believe, want, have(소유), see(이해)
✅현재 완료 Present Perfect
용법: 경험, 완료, 결과, 계속 (현재와 연결)
have/has + p.p.
I have visited Paris twice. (경험)
She has just finished her work. (완료)
함께 쓰이는 부사: ever, never, just, already, yet, recently, for, since
⏳현재 완료진행 Present Perfect Continuous
용법: 과거부터 지금까지 계속 진행 중인 동작 (기간 강조)
have/has been + V-ing
I have been studying for 3 hours.
It has been raining since this morning.

과거 시제 Past Tenses

⏪과거 단순 Simple Past
용법: 과거의 특정 시점에 일어난 일 (현재와 단절)
V-ed / 불규칙
I saw the movie last night.
함께 쓰이는 부사: yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago, then
🎬과거 진행 Past Continuous
용법: 과거 특정 시점에 진행 중, 과거 배경 서술
was/were + V-ing
I was sleeping when he called.
While she was cooking, the phone rang.
🔙과거 완료 Past Perfect
용법: 과거의 한 시점보다 더 이전에 일어난 일 (대과거)
had + p.p.
When I arrived, she had already left.
He had never seen snow before he came to Korea.
♾️과거 완료진행 Past Perfect Continuous
용법: 과거 특정 시점까지 계속 진행된 동작
had been + V-ing
She had been working for 5 hours when he called.

미래 시제 Future Tenses

형태용법예문
will + V즉흥적 결정, 예측, 약속I'll help you. / It will rain tomorrow.
be going to + V계획된 의도, 근거 있는 예측I'm going to study abroad. / Look at those clouds — it's going to rain.
will be + V-ing미래 특정 시점에 진행 중This time tomorrow, I will be flying to London.
will have + p.p.미래 특정 시점까지 완료될 일By Friday, I will have finished the report.
현재 진행형확정된 가까운 미래 계획We are meeting at 7 tonight.
현재 단순형시간표·일정 (열차·수업 등)The train leaves at 9 AM.
⚠️
시간·조건 부사절에서는 미래 대신 현재 시제를 씁니다!
When he arrives, we will start. (×When he will arrive)
If it rains tomorrow, I'll stay home. (×If it will rain)
📐 문장 형식 Sentence Patterns
영어의 5가지 기본 문형 — 모든 문장은 이 5형식 중 하나입니다.
형식구조예문주요 동사
1형식 S + V Birds fly. / The baby smiled. 자동사: go, come, run, sit, rise, exist
2형식 S + V + C She looks happy. / He became a doctor. 연결동사: be, look, seem, feel, become, get, grow, turn, remain, stay, smell, taste, sound
3형식 S + V + O I love music. / She wrote a letter. 타동사: love, eat, read, buy, make, see, hear
4형식 S + V + IO + DO He gave me a gift. / She told us the truth. 수여동사: give, send, teach, show, tell, buy, make, lend, bring
5형식 S + V + O + OC We call him Tom. / She made me happy. 목적격보어 동사: call, name, make, keep, find, leave, elect, consider, see, hear, have, let

4형식 → 3형식 전환 (전치사 변환)

동사4형식3형식 (전치사)
give, send, teach, show, lend, bring, write, offerI gave him a book.I gave a book to him.
buy, make, cook, get, findShe made me a cake.She made a cake for me.
askHe asked me a question.He asked a question of me.

5형식 목적격보어 패턴

보어 형태주요 동사예문
명사call, name, elect, appointWe call him Tom.
형용사make, keep, find, leave, driveMusic makes me happy.
to V (원형부정사)want, ask, tell, allow, expect, enableI want you to come.
V (동사원형)
사역동사		make, have, letShe made me clean the room.
V (동사원형)
지각동사		see, hear, feel, watch, noticeI saw him run across the street.
V-ing
진행 강조		see, hear, watch, feel, findI saw him running in the park.
p.p.
수동 관계		have, get, make, find, seeI had my car repaired. / I got my hair cut.
💡
사역동사 have vs get 차이:
I had him fix my car. (목적어가 행위자 → 원형)
I got him to fix my car. (get은 to 부정사 사용!)

🔧 조동사 Modal Verbs
조동사는 동사 앞에서 능력·허가·의무·추측 등 뜻을 더합니다. 뒤에 항상 동사원형이 옵니다.
조동사핵심 의미예문뉘앙스
can 능력 허가 가능성 She can speak French. / Can I use your phone? 현재 능력·비공식 허가 요청
could 과거 능력 공손한 허가 약한 가능성 I could swim when I was young. / Could you help me? can보다 정중, 가정법에서 사용
may 공식적 허가 약한 추측 May I come in? / It may rain tonight. 50% 이하 가능성, 공식적 허가 요청
might 아주 약한 추측 She might be at home. / I might go if I have time. may보다 더 낮은 가능성 (~30%)
will 의지·결심 강한 예측 I will help you. / It will be fine tomorrow. 화자의 의지·즉흥적 결정
would 과거 습관 공손한 요청 가정 He would often fish here. / Would you like tea? / I would travel if I were rich. will의 정중형·과거형, 가정법 필수
shall 제안 (I/We) 의무·운명 Shall we dance? / You shall not pass! 주로 Shall I/we로 제안·권유
should 충고·의무 기대 You should see a doctor. / He should be home by now. must보다 부드러운 의무·충고
must 강한 의무 강한 추측 You must wear a seatbelt. / She must be tired. 의무(=have to)·확신에 찬 추측(95%+)
have to 외부 의무 I have to submit this by Friday. 규칙·상황에 의한 의무 (must보다 객관적)
need not 불필요 You needn't worry. / You don't have to come. ~할 필요 없다 (금지 아님!)
must not 강한 금지 You must not smoke here. 절대 하면 안 됨 (≠ don't have to)
⚠️
must not vs don't have to 헷갈리지 않기!
You must not eat in class. → 먹으면 안 된다 (금지)
You don't have to eat in class. → 먹어도 되고 안 먹어도 된다 (불필요)

조동사 + have + p.p. (과거에 대한 추측·유감)

형태의미예문
must have p.p.~했음에 틀림없다 (강한 확신)She must have left already.
can't have p.p.~했을 리 없다 (강한 부정)He can't have done that.
may/might have p.p.~했을지도 모른다 (약한 추측)She might have missed the bus.
should have p.p.~했어야 했는데 (안 한 것에 후회·비난)You should have told me earlier.
could have p.p.~할 수 있었는데 (안 한 것)I could have helped you.
would have p.p.~했을 것이다 (가정법 과거완료)I would have gone if you had asked.
needn't have p.p.~할 필요 없었는데 (했음)You needn't have bought so much food.
🌀 가정법 Conditionals
if절로 조건을 제시하고 주절로 결과를 나타냅니다. 현실 여부에 따라 시제가 달라집니다.
종류if절주절의미
0형 가정법
Zero Conditional		 If + 현재 현재 항상 사실인 진리·습관
If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.
1형 가정법
First Conditional		 If + 현재 will/can/may + V 현재/미래의 실현 가능한 조건
If it rains, I will stay home.
2형 가정법
Second Conditional		 If + 과거 would/could/might + V 현재 사실과 반대되는 가정
If I were rich, I would travel the world.
3형 가정법
Third Conditional		 If + had p.p. would/could/might + have p.p. 과거 사실과 반대되는 가정 (후회·아쉬움)
If I had studied harder, I would have passed.
혼합 가정법
Mixed Conditional		 If + had p.p. (과거) would + V (현재) 과거 조건 + 현재 결과
If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now.
📌
2형 가정법에서 be동사는 인칭에 상관없이 were를 씁니다!
If I were you, I would apologize. (×If I was you)
단, 구어체에서 was를 쓰기도 하지만 시험에서는 were가 정답.

I wish / If only / as if (가정법 관용 표현)

표현의미예문
I wish + 과거현재 사실과 반대되는 소망I wish I had more time. (사실: I don't have much time)
I wish + had p.p.과거에 대한 후회I wish I had studied harder. (사실: I didn't study)
I wish + would V상대방의 변화 기원I wish you would stop smoking.
If onlyI wish의 강조형If only I were taller!
as if / as though마치 ~인 것처럼He acts as if he were the boss. / She talks as if she had seen it.
It's time + 과거지금쯤 ~해야 할 때It's time you went to bed.
would rather + 과거차라리 ~했으면 좋겠다I would rather you stayed home.

if 없는 가정법 (도치)

Were I rich, I would buy it.= If I were rich
Had she worked harder, she would have passed.= If she had worked harder
Should you need help, call me.= If you should need help (혹시 ~한다면)

① 수동태 Passive Voice

📌
행위자보다 행위의 대상을 강조할 때 사용. by + 행위자는 불필요하면 생략.
시제별 수동태 (be + p.p.)
시제능동태수동태
현재 단순She writes the report.The report is written (by her).
과거 단순He broke the window.The window was broken (by him).
미래They will finish the work.The work will be finished.
현재 진행She is writing the report.The report is being written.
현재 완료They have built the bridge.The bridge has been built.
조동사You must submit the form.The form must be submitted.
4형식 수동태 (목적어 2개)
능동태수동태 ①수동태 ②
They gave him a prize.He was given a prize.A prize was given to him.
She taught us English.We were taught English.English was taught to us.

② 관계사절 Relative Clauses

관계사선행사역할예문
who사람주격The man who called you is my friend.
whom사람목적격 (격식)The woman whom I met was kind.
whose사람·사물소유격The girl whose bag was stolen cried.
which사물·동물주격·목적격The car which I bought is blue.
that사람·사물주격·목적격 (제한적)The book that she likes is mine.
when시간명사관계부사I remember the day when we met.
where장소명사관계부사This is the city where I was born.
whyreason관계부사That's the reason why I left.
how선행사 없음관계부사This is how it works.
💡
제한적 vs 계속적 용법:
제한적: Students who study hard succeed. → 열심히 공부하는 학생들만
계속적: My brother, who lives in Seoul, called me. → 쉼표, 추가 정보 제공 (that 사용 불가)

③ to부정사 vs 동명사

패턴동사 목록예
to V만want, hope, wish, decide, plan, expect, need, promise, refuse, fail, agree, manageShe wants to go. / He decided to leave.
V-ing만enjoy, avoid, finish, stop, mind, keep, suggest, consider, practice, give up, put off, deny, admitShe enjoys reading. / He stopped smoking.
둘 다 가능 (의미 같음)like, love, hate, prefer, begin, start, continue, intendI like to swim. = I like swimming.
둘 다 가능 (의미 다름)remember, forget, regret, try, stop↓ 아래 참고
의미가 달라지는 동사
동사to V (미래 지향)V-ing (과거·사실)
remember기억해서 ~하다 (앞으로)~한 것을 기억하다 (과거)
예Remember to lock the door.I remember locking the door.
forget~할 것을 잊다~한 것을 잊다
예Don't forget to call me.I'll never forget meeting you.
try~하려고 노력하다시험삼아 ~해보다
예I tried to open the jar.Try adding more salt.
stop~하기 위해 멈추다~하는 것을 그만두다
예She stopped to buy coffee.She stopped buying coffee.
regret유감스럽게 ~하다~한 것을 후회하다
예I regret to tell you this.I regret saying that.

④ 분사구문 Participial Phrase

📌
부사절을 간결하게 줄이는 방법. 주절과 주어가 같을 때 접속사+주어 생략, 동사를 V-ing로.
의미부사절분사구문
시간When I arrived home, I ate dinner.Arriving home, I ate dinner.
이유Because she was tired, she went to bed.Being tired, she went to bed. / Tired, she went to bed.
조건If you turn left, you'll find the bank.Turning left, you'll find the bank.
동시As he sang, he played guitar.Singing, he played guitar.
완료After she had finished work, she left.Having finished work, she left.
수동Because it was written in English, I couldn't read it.Written in English, I couldn't read it.

⑥ 도치 Inversion

조건도치 예문일반 어순
Negative adv. 문두Never have I seen such a thing.I have never seen such a thing.
Only + 부사구 문두Only then did I understand.I understood only then.
Hardly / ScarcelyHardly had she left when it rained.She had hardly left when it rained.

⑦ 접속부사 vs 종속접속사

⚠️
접속부사(however, therefore 등)는 두 문장을 혼자 연결할 수 없습니다!
